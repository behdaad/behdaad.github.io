# WWII historical-basis audit

Reviewed 27 October 2026 against the owner's clarification: dramatization is acceptable, but the guide should help explain what actually happened. This is a screening of the historical basis and central premise of all 20 remaining selections, not certification of every scene or line of dialogue. IMDb scores were not rechecked in this audit; their 26 October snapshot is unchanged.

**Result:** no remaining selection deliberately substitutes a different course or outcome for the war's major historical events. Retain all 20. Several are fictional stories within real campaigns, and that is different from a reconstruction of a documented incident. In particular, Pearl Harbor and Enemy at the Gates are weak standalone history lessons despite being outside the alternate-history genre. Their visible learning notes now make that explicit. Fury and The Bridge on the River Kwai also identify their invented central engagements before the expandable notes.

The classifications below are editorial assessments of the sources, not numerical accuracy rankings. A producer's synopsis establishes intended subject and plot; museum/history sources provide historical context and corrections. Earlier chronology sources remain in `data.json`.

| Selection | Historical basis and limitation | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Gathering Storm | Biographical drama about Churchill's prewar warnings. Dialogue and context are rearranged; its jump after 1936 leaves major prewar events out. | [International Churchill Society](https://winstonchurchill.org/resources/in-the-media/film-reviews/the-gathering-storm/) |
| City of Life and Death | Drama within the real Nanjing occupation and massacre. Historical and fictional characters are combined; it is not an eyewitness transcript. | [San Sebastián Festival synopsis](https://www.sansebastianfestival.com/2009/sections_and_films/official_selection/7/570396/in) |
| Schindler’s List | Documented rescue dramatised on screen. The lists and their preparation were more complex than the film suggests; the real rescue is not an invented outcome. | [USHMM: Oskar Schindler](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/oskar-schindler) |
| Dunkirk | Real evacuation, fictionalised characters and incidents. Interwoven time scales do not represent an alternative outcome. | [BFI review](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/reviews/film-week-dunkirk-christopher-nolans-victorious-retreat) |
| Das Boot | Fictionalised U-boat narrative adapted from Buchheim's experience. Atmosphere and service conditions do not make it a literal patrol record. | [BFI: war-at-sea films](https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-battle-sea-films) |
| Tora! Tora! Tora! | Historical reconstruction of Pearl Harbor preparations and attack. Reconstructed dialogue includes an unverified closing Yamamoto quotation. | [Naval History: film comparison](https://www.usni.org/magazines/naval-history-magazine/2001/june/pearl-harbor-comes-big-screen) |
| Pearl Harbor | Invented romance and pilots within the real attack and Doolittle Raid. Significant military and chronological distortions make this optional spectacle; prefer Tora for learning the attack. | [Naval History critique](https://www.usni.org/magazines/naval-history-magazine/2001/august/pearl-harbor-bombed-again), [production/script history](https://www.usni.org/magazines/naval-history-magazine/2001/june/pearl-harbor-comes-big-screen) |
| Midway (2019) | Real naval campaign and principal figures. Compressed operations, effects and altered characterisation, notably Dick Best, need caveats. | [National WWII Museum review](https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/midway-2019-different-take) |
| The Pacific | Historical Marine campaign drama, drawing on veterans' experiences and memoirs. Reconstructed personal scenes are not verbatim testimony. | [Amblin](https://amblin.com/tv/the-pacific/), [Writers Guild source credits](https://www.wgaeast.org/wp-content/uploads/typo3/user_upload/files/awards-journal/AwardsJournal2011.pdf) |
| Enemy at the Gates | Real Stalingrad and Zaitsev; the central duel is unverified and the romance invented. Do not learn representative Soviet tactics or the actual battle's course from the sniper plot. | [Military History Matters, Taylor Downing review](https://the-past.com/review/tv-film/war-on-film-enemy-at-the-gates/) |
| Hacksaw Ridge | Doss and the Okinawa rescues are historical. Courtship, training and events are condensed; earlier campaigns are omitted. | [Desmond Doss Council FAQ](https://www.desmonddoss.com/hacksaw-ridge-movie/faq.php) |
| The Great Escape | Real 1944 breakout, with reshaped participants, compressed chronology and invented motorcycle action. | [RAF historical account](https://www.raf.mod.uk/news/articles/great-escape-80/), [AFI film record](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/23871-THE-GREATESCAPE) |
| The Bridge on the River Kwai | Fiction based in the real railway forced-labour system. Nicholson, the mission and the film's destruction are not the real bridge history or a fair portrait of Toosey. This local invented plot does not posit an alternative course for the war; it still requires a prominent caveat for this guide. | [Sony Pictures Museum: novel adaptation](https://www.sonypicturesmuseum.com/collection/film/603/bridge-on-the-river-kwai-the.html), [TCM: real bridges versus film](https://www.tcm.com/articles/afi-top-100/24052/the-bridge-on-the-river-kwai-1957) |
| Patton | Biographical campaign drama, with simplified rivalries and reconstructed personal scenes. | [AFI](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/23463-PATTON), [studio synopsis](https://www.20thcenturystudios.com/movies/patton) |
| Masters of the Air | Historical 100th Bomb Group narrative. Personal scenes and pacing remain dramatic reconstructions. | [National WWII Museum and historical consultant discussion](https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/topics/masters-air) |
| Band of Brothers | Historical Easy Company campaign narrative, with compressed incidents and imperfect individual portrayals. | [Amblin: adaptation of Ambrose's history](https://amblin.com/tv/band-of-brothers/) |
| Saving Private Ryan | Real Normandy invasion, invented squad, rescue mission and Ramelle battle. The mission is not a documented D-Day operation. | [Amblin: D-Day and rescue plot](https://amblin.com/movie/saving-private-ryan/) |
| Downfall | Historical collapse of the regime and bunker setting, reconstructed from accounts rather than recordings. | [BFI](https://player.bfi.org.uk/subscription/film/watch-downfall-2004-online) |
| Fury | Invented crew and engagements within the real April 1945 invasion of Germany. The crossroads defence does not document a named historical battle. | [Sony's official premise](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/fury), [contemporary review of the invented narrative](https://time.com/3509916/fury-brad-pitt-world-war-ii-movie-review/) |
| Grave of the Fireflies | Semi-autobiographical fiction within real incendiary bombing and civilian deprivation. The siblings' story is not a literal biography or an account of the atomic bombings. | [BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/studio-ghibli-five-essential-films), [BFI Film Classics programme extract](https://bfidatadigipres.github.io/anime/2022/05/19/grave-of-the-fireflies/) |

No further removals or replacements were made: the owner's allowance for dramatization includes invented individuals and missions inside real history. The absence of alternate history does not imply equal usefulness for learning; visible notes distinguish these cases. Historical event cards and sources remain the factual reference alongside each film.
