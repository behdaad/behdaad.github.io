HOW TO READ THIS

Stories overlap.
History keeps moving.

Story dates and release dates are different things. A coverage span tells you when the main plot takes place. Event links explain what that story helps you understand; a span alone does not promise that every event is depicted.

A gap means a gap: separate story periods stay separate. Viewing numbers suggest a useful order, even when their years overlap.
