Choose a plan, check dinner for five, then check the bar’s booking or walk-in policy. Ask about dietary needs before reserving. Suggested arrival times are a plan, not a reservation.

Fitz’s sets the mood for this guide, but its location in Oost stretches the city-centre brief. Oldenhof is the closest cosy alternative here.
