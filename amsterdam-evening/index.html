BEFORE THE GROUP SAYS “BOOK IT”

A small bit of planning.

Published hours are shown as a planning reference. Check them directly before going. Esh’s reported hours come from editorial listings; confirm them directly before choosing this plan. Current availability, private events and last-minute changes still need confirmation.

Choose a plan, check current availability, then check the bar’s booking or walk-in policy. Ask about dietary needs before reserving. Suggested arrival times are a plan, not a reservation.

Fitz’s sets the mood for this guide, but its location in Oost stretches the city-centre brief. Oldenhof is the closest cosy alternative here. Stacks, KID, Pendergast and Morgan & Mees extend the shortlist west of Centrum; Bar Mokum and Esh add a southern option. The Pendergast and Dutch Courage–Tujuh Maret plans exceed the original 20-minute walking brief.
