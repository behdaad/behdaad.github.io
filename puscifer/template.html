00 / Orientation

Leave a little room
for the absurd.

Puscifer’s creative core is Maynard James Keenan, Mat Mitchell and Carina Round. The songs, theatrical concerts, animations and comedy belong to the same playground. The band’s own era-by-era credits show how many collaborators pass through it.

The lore is deliberately elastic: Billy D’s misadventures, a suspicious agency and a new goth cast overlap without a single tidy timeline. This guide follows release order, with prequels and reposts identified. The short descriptions are listening prompts and story signposts, not a claim that every image has one official meaning.

Start with the {{ESSENTIALS}} essentials if you want a manageable route before the show. Add the rest as curiosity takes over.

What is included — and how exhaustive is this?

The official homepage, news, media, discography, all 18 sitemap-listed pages, the indexed news article, and all 97 current product records were reviewed for relevant media.
