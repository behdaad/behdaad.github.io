Booking.com · iOS

Overview

I worked on a horizontal comparison experience for the room list in the Booking.com iOS app. The project explored how to make the differences between available rooms easier to understand without asking travellers to repeatedly move back and forth between separate options.

The problem

Room options often share most of their details while differing in a few important ways: bed configuration, occupancy, cancellation policy, included meals, and price. In a vertical list, those differences can be difficult to keep in mind while making a decision.

The approach

The horizontal layout keeps multiple room options in the same context, so travellers can scan comparable details and move between choices directly. The implementation had to preserve the amount of useful information in each room card while making the direction of scrolling and the current position clear.

Accessibility

Accessibility was part of the interaction design rather than a follow-up. The experience needed a meaningful reading order, understandable room and price information, predictable focus movement, and clear announcements when travellers moved between options.
