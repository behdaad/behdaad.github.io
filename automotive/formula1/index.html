CURATION & PROVENANCE

A viewing guide, with a paper trail.

This is a curated route through the history, not a ranking of every race. Championship clinchers and deciders provide the spine; celebrated races, technical changes and consequential controversies supply the detours. The modern era receives more space. Results are spoilers by default; “Hide race outcomes” lets you reveal race stories individually.

Documentaries and screen stories are placed by the period covered, rather than their release date. A career retrospective may reveal later events. Portraits are reference photographs and may have been taken years after the introduction shown here. Film and drama results are not historical race results.

Selection sources, archive limitations & image credits

Race selections draw on Formula 1’s greatest-races and championship-showdown retrospectives, the F1 TV archive and documentary catalog, and official race results. Each race’s notes link to its result and a historical account with further references. Selected rulings have an additional primary source.
