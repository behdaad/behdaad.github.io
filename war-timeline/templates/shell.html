HOW TO READ THIS

One story can cross many years.

Each year lists the films whose main story takes place then. The same title can appear in several years and beside several events; it still has one full entry in the viewing guide. A connection labelled “Context” or “Background” does not mean the film depicts that event.

On each film’s year strip, coloured cells mark main-story coverage and pale cells mark years outside it. Gaps stay visible. Brief prologues, framing interviews and later-life epilogues are excluded. IMDb scores describe audience reception, not historical accuracy.
